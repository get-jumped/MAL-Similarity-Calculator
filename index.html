Enter two or more MyAnimeList usernames to compare their libraries. You'll see which series everyone has watched, what only one person has seen, and how the scores line up on shared titles. Useful for picking your next watch from a friend's list, or for finding out how far apart your tastes really are.

  • Unique titles nobody else on the list has seen
  • Filter by status: watching, completed, on hold, dropped
